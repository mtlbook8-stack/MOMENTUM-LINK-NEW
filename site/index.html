01 — The problem

A specification is hard to picture. A demo is not.

Written requirements get signed off long before anyone can see what they will actually get. Everyone reads the same document differently, and the gap only shows up late — when changing it is expensive.

So we put a working demo in front of you early, correct it against your notes, and build once you agree it is right. The disagreements happen while they are still cheap.
